Day 01

Historic Site Exploration & Coastal Stroll

13:30 PM

Fort San Domingo

One of the most iconic attractions in Tamsui. This vibrant red fortress carries over 300 years of history. The compound is well-maintained and offers an expansive view overlooking the Tamsui River, making it an excellent spot to explore and take pictures with classmates.

Classic Landmark Grade-A Historic Site
15:00 PM

Hobe Fort

A historic military defense fortress preserved from the Qing Dynasty, featuring the inscription "Key to the North Gate" written by Liu Ming-chuan above the entrance. Walking through the thick stone walls and tunnels gives a strong sense of history, surrounded by a peaceful, lush green environment.

Historic Fortress National Monument
16:30 PM

Shalun Beach

Featuring a wide and gentle shoreline, this beach is a favored spot for catching the sunset in Tamsui. The atmosphere here is remarkably leisurely and quiet, perfect for relaxing by the coast, enjoying the sea breeze, and walking while chatting.

Coastal Walk Sunset Viewing
18:30 PM

Fisherman's Wharf

Strolling along the extensive wooden boardwalk offers a clear view of the illuminated Lover's Bridge and the night river scape. As evening sets in, the harbor lights up beautifully, making it an ideal place to grab a drink, sample local snacks, and unwind.

Night View Spot Leisure Harbor
Day 02

Old Street Food Discovery & Waterfront Cycling

11:30 AM

Tamsui Old Street

After checkout, head to the bustling Old Street commercial district. The area brings together a rich variety of famous local treats, such as A-gei, fish ball soup, shrimp rolls, and iron eggs, allowing you to sample authentic delicacies while experiencing the lively town environment.

Local Cuisine Historic Market
14:30 PM

Tamshui Gold Seashore

After a satisfying meal, it is highly recommended to rent a YouBike and enjoy a pleasant ride along the waterfront cycling path. You can look out over the expansive Tamsui River and Guanyin Mountain while catching a refreshing breeze, concluding the journey on a highly relaxing note.

Riverside Cycling Waterfront Scenery
